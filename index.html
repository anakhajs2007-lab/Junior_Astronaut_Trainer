🛰️ Mission Actions

Each day consumes supplies. Save Water reduces daily water use. Refilling water costs power. Shielding protects against a solar radiation event when one occurs.

Tip: Actions can be used between days. Shielding only helps during a radiation event and costs power to activate.

🧠 Mission Learning

Why manage resources? A lunar crew cannot quickly call for a delivery, so oxygen, water, food, and power must be carefully rationed.

Why shielding? Space radiation can be more intense during solar particle events. A habitat's shielding and good mission planning help reduce exposure.

Learning tip: conserve supplies early so the crew has a safety margin later in the mission.
